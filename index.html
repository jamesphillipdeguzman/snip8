snip8 Workflow & User Guide

Step-by-step instructions for desktop sync bridge, live tracking, mobile stop, and compliance.

1

Setup Desktop Sync Bridge

Create a browser bookmark named Sync Bridge in Chrome or Edge, and paste the bookmarklet URL below.

• Zero extensions or permissions required.
• Injects a temporary green badge (Sync Bridge Connected).
• Runs ambiently every 10s to capture remote stop requests.
2

Starting a Work Session

  • a. Start your timer in Springboard at your shift beginning.
  • b. Click the Sync Bridge bookmark once in the active Springboard tab.
  • c. Capture your timesheet crop (Win + Shift + S) and paste into snip8.
  • d. Ensure Live Increment is toggled ON to tick upward in sync.
3

Tracking & Remote Stop

  • • Open snip8 on mobile using your same session PIN.
  • • Tap 🔄 Sync to pull cloud progress; wall-clock drift is reconciled automatically.
  • • Tap ⏹️ Stop Springboard from anywhere. Your desktop Springboard timer halts automatically within 10s.
4

Policy & Compliance Rules

Target: 8h 00m (28,800s) — The optimal stop point for all shifts.
5-Minute Grace Window (8h 00m – 8h 05m): Permitted without disciplinary breach, but extra minutes are unpaid.
> 8h 05m Overtime: Formal policy violation. Stop timer immediately.
